# Orio Builders - Maple Project Landing Page & Pitch Strategy

- **Client**: Orio Builders
- **Project**: ORIO MAPLE (Completed Luxury Villa & Apartment Project)
- **Tagline**: "Art of Peaceful Living"
- **Location**: Kizhakkan Muthoor, Thiruvalla, Pathanamthitta, Kerala (250m off Kizhakkan Muthoor Jn)
- **Units**: 11 Exclusive Luxury Units
- **Permit Number**: H11/Ba/83719/19 Dtd: 20/03/2022
- **RERA Reg Number**: K-Rera/Prj/800/2022

## Design System & Theme
- **Color Palette**: Clean Refreshing White (`#FFFFFF`, `#F8FAFC`) + Deep Navy Blue (`#0B192C`, `#1E3A8A`) + Emerald Leaf Accents (`#10B981`, `#059669`).
- **Typography**: Playfair Display (Serif Headings) + Plus Jakarta Sans (Clean Modern Sans).
- **Layout Architecture**: Single page, responsive, image-rich visual cards, reduced text clutter, integrated inquiry cards (no clunky generic buttons).
- **Media Assets**: Utilizes authentic project screenshot assets from `public/assets/orion_images/` (`maple1.png` through `maple6.png`).

## Page Sections
1. **Header & Brand Navigation**: ORIO MAPLE logo, location badge, contact link.
2. **Hero Section**: Feature image (`maple1.png`), title, concise description, quick facts badges.
3. **Project Specifications Grid**: Official RERA, Permit, Unit count, and location metrics.
4. **Nature & Peaceful Living Showcase**: Interactive cards featuring images from `orion_images/` (`maple2.png`, `maple3.png`, `maple4.png`).
5. **Authentic Project Photo Gallery**: Grid of all project assets from `public/assets/orion_images/` with lightbox view.
6. **Location & Accessibility Matrix**: Distance breakdown to Thiruvalla Railway Station, hospitals, airport.
7. **Integrated Client Inquiry Card**: Contact form payload for site visits & pitch inquiries.
8. **Footer**: Official Orio Builders contact info and links.
